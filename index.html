Learn: School Mascot

Students voted for a new school mascot: a tiger, a shark or an eagle. The results show that 4 out of every 10 voters chose the tiger.

A percent is a ratio, or rate, that compares a number with 100. You can write a percent as a fraction with a denominator of 100, or with the percent sign %.

In words40 out of 100
As a fraction40100
As a percent40%

Pick a model. Each one shows the tiger votes.

Hundredths grid: each square is 1%. Tenths grid: each strip is 10%. 410 = 40100 = 40%

Now you shade it. Drag the mouse to shade many squares at once.

Pattern spotter
Every square is 1% and every column is 10%. So 23% is 2 columns and 3 squares: 20% + 3% = 23%.

Learn: Shanice’s Kittens

Shanice’s cat had two kittens, a grey kitten and an orange kitten. The grey kitten weighs 4 ounces. The orange kitten weighs 1.5 times as much as the grey kitten.

A tape diagram can show the two weights. The grey kitten’s weight is the whole: one bar of 10 equal parts, so each part is 10%.

Summarize: Understand Percent

  1. A percent is a ratio, or rate, that compares a number with 100.
  2. A percent can be written with the percent sign, %.
  3. A percent can be greater than 100%. Then it is more than one whole.

Reflect

How are percents less than 100% and percents greater than 100% alike? How are they different?

See an example answer

Both compare a number with 100. Both can be shown on hundredths grids or tape diagrams. A percent less than 100% is less than one whole, so only part of one grid is shaded. A percent greater than 100% is more than one whole, so I need more than one grid. For example, 130% is one full grid and 30 squares of another grid.
